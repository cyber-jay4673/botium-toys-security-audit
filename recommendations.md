# Botium Toys Security Recommendations

## Priority 1: Protect Sensitive Customer Data

### Implement Encryption

Botium Toys should implement encryption for customer credit card information, particularly when data is stored or transmitted.

**Reason:** Encryption helps protect sensitive information from unauthorized access and supports payment-card security requirements.

### Restrict Access to Sensitive Data

Implement least-privilege access controls so employees only have access to the information required for their job responsibilities.

**Reason:** Limiting access reduces the potential impact of compromised accounts and insider threats.

## Priority 2: Improve Account Security

### Strengthen Password Requirements

Update the password policy to require stronger passwords and establish appropriate minimum complexity requirements.

### Implement Centralized Password Management

Deploy a centralized password management system that can enforce password requirements and simplify secure password recovery.

### Implement Separation of Duties

Separate sensitive responsibilities between different employees or roles.

**Reason:** Separation of duties reduces the risk associated with compromised accounts and malicious insider activity.

## Priority 3: Improve Business Continuity

### Implement Regular Backups

Establish regular backups of critical business data and verify that backups can be successfully restored.

### Develop a Disaster Recovery Plan

Create and maintain a disaster recovery plan covering critical systems and business processes.

**Reason:** Backups and disaster recovery procedures improve the organization's ability to recover from security incidents, system failures, or other disruptive events.

## Priority 4: Improve Security Monitoring

### Deploy an Intrusion Detection System

Implement an IDS to identify suspicious or anomalous network activity.

### Establish a Legacy-System Maintenance Schedule

Create a documented schedule for monitoring, maintaining, and updating legacy systems.

**Reason:** Regular maintenance helps identify and manage vulnerabilities associated with outdated systems.

## Priority 5: Improve Asset Management

### Establish an Asset Inventory

Create and maintain an accurate inventory of hardware, software, systems, data, and other relevant assets.

### Classify Sensitive Data

Classify data according to its sensitivity and business importance.

**Reason:** Proper asset inventory and data classification help the organization determine which assets require stronger security controls.

## Conclusion

Botium Toys has several foundational controls already in place, including a firewall, antivirus software, CCTV surveillance, physical locks, and fire detection systems.

However, the organization should prioritize protection of sensitive customer data, access control, business continuity, authentication, security monitoring, and asset management.

Implementing these recommendations would help reduce the organization's overall risk and strengthen its security posture.
