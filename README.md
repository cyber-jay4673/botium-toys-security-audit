# Botium Toys Security Audit

## Project Overview

This project documents an internal security audit conducted for Botium Toys, a fictional toy company with a physical storefront, warehouse, office, and growing online presence.

The audit evaluates the organization's existing security controls and compliance practices and identifies areas that require improvement.

## Audit Objectives

* Assess the security controls currently implemented by Botium Toys.
* Identify gaps in administrative, technical, and physical controls.
* Evaluate compliance practices related to PCI DSS, GDPR, and SOC.
* Identify risks to company assets, employees, and customer data.
* Recommend improvements to strengthen the organization's security posture.

## Scope

The audit covers the entire security program at Botium Toys, including:

* IT systems and software
* Employee devices
* Internal network and internet access
* Customer and company data
* E-commerce and inventory systems
* Physical security
* Data retention and storage
* Legacy systems
* Security policies and procedures

## Methodology

The assessment was based on:

1. Reviewing the organization's scope, goals, and risk assessment report.
2. Reviewing the defined cybersecurity control categories.
3. Assessing administrative, technical, and physical controls.
4. Evaluating compliance best practices.
5. Identifying security gaps and associated risks.
6. Developing recommendations for improving the organization's security posture.

## Key Findings

The assessment identified several significant security weaknesses, including:

* Lack of least-privilege access controls
* Lack of separation of duties
* Weak password requirements
* No centralized password management system
* No intrusion detection system
* No encryption for stored customer credit card information
* No backups of critical data
* No disaster recovery plan
* Inadequate asset management and classification
* Inconsistent legacy-system maintenance procedures

Existing controls included a firewall, antivirus software, CCTV surveillance, physical locks, and fire detection/prevention systems.

## Risk Assessment

The organization's reported overall risk score was **8 out of 10**, indicating a relatively high level of risk.

The most significant concerns involve unauthorized access to sensitive information, protection of customer payment information, business continuity, and compliance with applicable regulations.

## Compliance Areas Reviewed

The audit reviewed best practices related to:

* Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI DSS)
* General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)
* System and Organization Controls (SOC)

## Recommendations

Key recommendations include:

* Implement least-privilege access controls.
* Implement separation of duties.
* Strengthen password requirements.
* Deploy centralized password management.
* Implement encryption for sensitive customer information.
* Deploy an intrusion detection system.
* Establish regular data backups.
* Develop and test a disaster recovery plan.
* Improve asset inventory and data classification.
* Establish a regular maintenance schedule for legacy systems.
* Review and strengthen access control and account management policies.
